About the model

DigiGEM expands a plant primary-metabolism reconstruction with Digitalis-specific cardenolide biosynthesis and associated steroid, brassinosteroid, and terpenoid pathway updates. It supports constraint-based investigations of the metabolic cost of defense.

Objective function

The SBML objective combines biomass reaction BIO_L (weight 693) with Add_Card13_c, Add_Card14_c, and Add_Card15_c (weight 100 each). Consult the manuscript for context.

Reproducibility

The model is distributed as SBML and MATLAB files. MATLAB and the COBRA Toolbox with a configured solver are needed for the supplied demo. The scripts have not been independently run during website preparation.
